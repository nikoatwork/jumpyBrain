# Fresh ordering/read boundary confirmation

Fifteen fictional Markdown pages and twelve reserved tasks, authored by a separate fixture agent before retrieval outputs. Only `corpus/` is indexed. `queries.json` is public interaction input; `rubric.json` is scoring-only and must never choose candidates or reads. `../build-boundary-confirmation.py` regenerates the authored bytes and validates every required clause against its declared source. `AUTHOR-FREEZE.json` records the pre-output file identities.

The two domains are a shared ceramics kiln, Kilnwick, and a neighborhood cargo-cycle lending trial, Pedalbrook. They share a volunteer fair but have distinct coordinators and operating decisions. Both sometimes use the alias Hub; that alias does not establish shared authority. Scope, evidence dates, proposal status and named responsibility are explicit. The ceramics and cycle details are fictional scenario facts, not practical safety guidance.

The notes vary in length and structure: two multi-section guides place purpose and coordinator information below the opening paragraphs; shorter records cover inspections, incoming/outgoing handovers, proposed extensions, an original plan and a financial/borrowing log. The tasks include orientation, rationale and multi-source workflow questions, plus protected exact-title navigation, quoted wording, historical evidence, unknown approval and two named groups. A source being available or a name matching is insufficient without the governing qualification.

The author knew the declared mechanisms under test: named metadata partition across the existing grouped pool, and first-two-plus-final-available follow-up selection. The author did not inspect `.runs/`, retrieval outputs, candidate ranks or private memory and did not tailor facts to observed gains. Sources, questions and initial rubric share one author; a different model agent's review is not independent human adjudication. Once any comparison is inspected, these cases become seen controls.

Required spans are literal source-bound diagnostics. They do not measure answer generation, semantic entailment, independent abstention, human usability, large-corpus recall or real-world operating costs. Pages remain modest in size despite the longer guides; no guarantee follows that a whole document fits a guarded response envelope. Preserve every miss and do not edit sources, questions or thresholds after observing quality outputs.
