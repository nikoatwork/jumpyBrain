## Policy and confirmation procedure

The pure eval policy uses one literal capitalized word that is either the first word of two distinct canonical titles or a declared single-word alias. It stable-partitions the first 40 existing grouped results by that title-prefix/tag/alias match. Multiple recognized names do nothing. Exact-title navigation, constrained queries and explicit temporal requests retain original selection. No corpus-specific names, body inference, discovery, new model, state, index or larger output budget is added. Names are relevance hints, never proof of approval or current truth.

The [v3 contract](../IDENTITY-EXPERIMENT.md) was frozen before the Selkie ferry/Tamar reservoir outputs. Their baseline already completed seven of eight tasks; the remaining Selkie current-state query used a constrained compound and returned no evidence in either arm. This unsuccessful confirmation remains in the record. The policy bytes were then kept fixed while a [single final extension](../IDENTITY-CONFIRMATION.md) added Belltower repair cafe, Copperfin paddling and Mothlight gallery access, each with five pages and four tasks. All earlier cases and failures were retained. Thresholds were not relaxed, no policy tuning used these outputs, and no further extension was attempted. Repeated confirmation and same-author questions/judgments still limit confidence.

The final three fresh orientation tasks all lack purpose/owner support. Their unchanged compact discovery has 40 raw candidates / 38 grouped results and contains each accepted scope decision, but not its necessary project introduction. Identity ordering promotes the decision without discovering the brief. The names Inez, Dev and Noor and the requested project purpose are unavailable from the displayed target-source packets. This is a genuine query-bound evidence gap, not merely a failed preferred phrase. All nine other fresh tasks already pass and remain passing. The correct next mechanism to investigate is discovery coverage for named subjects in long multi-clause queries, before more final-pool ordering.

Same-author review also confirmed real gains on controls: Juniper/Ridge/Ledger orientation combine their purpose/coordinator with governing approved scope; Vellum/Bracken/Quartz recover full handbooks; six scheduling cases gain the necessary site restriction beside their arrangement. No answer model ran, and no independent semantic aggregate is claimed. Verified provenance and response-budget failures are zero in both complete comparisons.
