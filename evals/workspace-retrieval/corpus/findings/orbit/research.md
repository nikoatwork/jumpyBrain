# What participants reported

Orbit learner interviews found that adults stayed away after an absence because they expected a public catch-up test in front of the group.

## Observation method

The coordinator collected a small convenience sample while participants attempted the normal process. The notes describe why some people stopped; they do not establish how often the problem occurs across the whole audience.

## Competing explanation

Staff initially suspected lack of interest. The observed friction suggests people may want the outcome but cannot complete the required steps. Separate these explanations in the next review rather than calling every incomplete attempt a rejection.

A useful review distinguishes what was observed from what the team hopes will happen. Keep the original account alongside the decision so a new contributor can challenge the reasoning. A change in wording is not evidence that the underlying problem has disappeared. Small groups can give useful direction without establishing a population-wide result.
