# Measurement note

For Mosaic, measure room enquiries resolved within two offered callback windows. Count attempts that did not finish, not just the people who volunteer praise after the event.

## Interpretation

The first small trial is a feasibility observation. It cannot establish a stable effect size across all users or seasons. Keep the denominator and any change in who participated.

## Failure modes

Beware that fewer incoming calls can indicate abandonment rather than successful booking. Do not choose a proxy just because a tool can count it automatically.

A useful review distinguishes what was observed from what the team hopes will happen. Keep the original account alongside the decision so a new contributor can challenge the reasoning. A change in wording is not evidence that the underlying problem has disappeared. Small groups can give useful direction without establishing a population-wide result.
