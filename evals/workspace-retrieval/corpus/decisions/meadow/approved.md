# Accepted decision

The September 18 decision approves named weekly watering pairs with an explicit backup for Meadow, starting on October 22.

## Why this approach

Meadow chose named weekly pairs because a clear backup can cover an absence without asking the same volunteer by default.

The decision log separates the accepted course of action from suggestions discussed in the room. Suggestions remain valuable context even when they are not selected. Reopening the decision requires a recorded reason and confirmation from the responsible coordinator. Copying a paragraph into a later note does not itself grant new approval.

## Scope and conditions

Meadow paired watering covers the shared beds only; private allotment plots remain each holder's responsibility.

The coordinator must review the agreed measure before expanding the audience. A later suggestion, calendar edit or copied announcement does not extend this approval.
