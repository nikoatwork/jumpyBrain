# Original approach

The September 4 Meadow plan was an open chat asking whoever is free to water, starting on October 8.

## Reason at the time

The initial assumption was that using the familiar process would minimize explanation and let the team start quickly. Nobody had yet observed whether a person with a limited time window could finish it.

The decision log separates the accepted course of action from suggestions discussed in the room. Suggestions remain valuable context even when they are not selected. Reopening the decision requires a recorded reason and confirmation from the responsible coordinator. Copying a paragraph into a later note does not itself grant new approval.

## Review trigger

Revisit after the pilot if participants fail to complete the important step. This document records the September 4 plan and must not be read as the latest instruction after a later decision is accepted.
