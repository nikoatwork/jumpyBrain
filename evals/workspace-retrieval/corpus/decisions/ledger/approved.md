# Accepted decision

The September 18 decision approves separate received and accepted invoice messages for Ledger, starting on October 22.

## Why this approach

Ledger chose separate received and accepted messages because a technical upload cannot establish that an invoice is approved for payment.

The decision log separates the accepted course of action from suggestions discussed in the room. Suggestions remain valuable context even when they are not selected. Reopening the decision requires a recorded reason and confirmation from the responsible coordinator. Copying a paragraph into a later note does not itself grant new approval.

## Scope and conditions

Ledger message changes cover translator invoices only; employee expense claims stay on the existing approval route.

The coordinator must review the agreed measure before expanding the audience. A later suggestion, calendar edit or copied announcement does not extend this approval.

[Participant research](findings/ledger/research.md) and [partner constraint](notes/ledger/customer.md) explain the problem. [Original plan](decisions/ledger/initial.md) remains available for historical questions.
