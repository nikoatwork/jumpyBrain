# Accepted decision

The September 18 decision approves a private five-minute recap before the class for Orbit, starting on October 22.

## Why this approach

Orbit chose a private five-minute recap because learners can rejoin the ongoing class without exposing what they missed.

The decision log separates the accepted course of action from suggestions discussed in the room. Suggestions remain valuable context even when they are not selected. Reopening the decision requires a recorded reason and confirmation from the responsible coordinator. Copying a paragraph into a later note does not itself grant new approval.

## Scope and conditions

Orbit private recaps are approved for the adult evening cohort only; the weekend youth group is outside this trial.

The coordinator must review the agreed measure before expanding the audience. A later suggestion, calendar edit or copied announcement does not extend this approval.

[Participant research](findings/orbit/research.md) and [partner constraint](notes/orbit/customer.md) explain the problem. [Original plan](decisions/orbit/initial.md) remains available for historical questions.
