# Accepted decision

The September 18 decision approves short object labels and an optional multilingual audio route for Lantern, starting on October 22.

## Why this approach

Lantern chose short object labels with an optional audio route because visitors can begin exploring before choosing a language.

The decision log separates the accepted course of action from suggestions discussed in the room. Suggestions remain valuable context even when they are not selected. Reopening the decision requires a recorded reason and confirmation from the responsible coordinator. Copying a paragraph into a later note does not itself grant new approval.

## Scope and conditions

Lantern evening access is approved for the school preview only; general after-hours opening still needs a staffing review.

The coordinator must review the agreed measure before expanding the audience. A later suggestion, calendar edit or copied announcement does not extend this approval.

[Participant research](findings/lantern/research.md) and [partner constraint](notes/lantern/customer.md) explain the problem. [Original plan](decisions/lantern/initial.md) remains available for historical questions.
