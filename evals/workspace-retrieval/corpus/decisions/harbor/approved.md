# Accepted decision

The September 18 decision approves a three-field welcome screen followed by optional invitations for Harbor, starting on October 22.

## Why this approach

Harbor chose progressive setup because a coordinator can invite the rest of the team after completing one useful task.

The decision log separates the accepted course of action from suggestions discussed in the room. Suggestions remain valuable context even when they are not selected. Reopening the decision requires a recorded reason and confirmation from the responsible coordinator. Copying a paragraph into a later note does not itself grant new approval.

## Scope and conditions

Harbor pilot invitations are limited to Alder Trust; public self-service remains blocked until the support handoff is reviewed.

The coordinator must review the agreed measure before expanding the audience. A later suggestion, calendar edit or copied announcement does not extend this approval.

[Participant research](findings/harbor/research.md) and [partner constraint](notes/harbor/customer.md) explain the problem. [Original plan](decisions/harbor/initial.md) remains available for historical questions.
