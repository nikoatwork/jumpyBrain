# Accepted decision

The September 18 decision approves moderated question cards read aloud by the host for Forum, starting on October 22.

## Why this approach

Forum chose moderated question cards because the host can read each question into the same microphone used for remote speakers.

The decision log separates the accepted course of action from suggestions discussed in the room. Suggestions remain valuable context even when they are not selected. Reopening the decision requires a recorded reason and confirmation from the responsible coordinator. Copying a paragraph into a later note does not itself grant new approval.

## Scope and conditions

Forum question cards are approved for the remote-speaker track only; in-person workshops retain their own facilitation.

The coordinator must review the agreed measure before expanding the audience. A later suggestion, calendar edit or copied announcement does not extend this approval.

[Participant research](findings/forum/research.md) and [partner constraint](notes/forum/customer.md) explain the problem. [Original plan](decisions/forum/initial.md) remains available for historical questions.
