# Exceptions register

No signed approval for extending Orbit beyond its agreed scope is recorded in this workspace; the approver is unknown.

## What is known

The existing decision has a specific audience and operational boundary. A request to expand it is not a signed exception. Absence from this fictional workspace cannot prove that no discussion occurred elsewhere.

## What to do next

Ask Eli for the missing record before naming an approver or promising broader coverage. Keep uncertainty visible in any summary.

A useful review distinguishes what was observed from what the team hopes will happen. Keep the original account alongside the decision so a new contributor can challenge the reasoning. A change in wording is not evidence that the underlying problem has disappeared. Small groups can give useful direction without establishing a population-wide result.
