# Purpose

Orbit aims to help adult learners return after missing a weekly numeracy class.

Orbit is coordinated by Eli.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that a missed lesson causes learners to wait until the next course instead of rejoining. The work concerns community tutoring, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is returning learners attending their next two sessions. Watch for a misleading improvement: a busy registration list can hide repeated absence after a missed lesson. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
