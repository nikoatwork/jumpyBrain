# Purpose

Juniper aims to serve a reliable weekday lunch to nearby shift workers without increasing kitchen waste.

Juniper is coordinated by Sana.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that customers with a short break cannot predict when a cooked-to-order lunch will arrive. The work concerns restaurant lunch service, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is orders collected within the promised ten-minute window. Watch for a misleading improvement: a larger number of orders can hide longer waits and discarded food. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
