# Purpose

Ledger aims to help freelance translators understand when a customer has accepted their invoice.

Ledger is coordinated by Ravi.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that an upload receipt looks like payment approval even when an invoice is awaiting review. The work concerns freelance invoice administration, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is translators correctly distinguishing receipt from acceptance. Watch for a misleading improvement: a high email open rate can hide the same mistaken belief about payment approval. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
