# Ledger

These instructions are for the accepted trial. Other groups may use a superficially similar process under a different agreement.

## Before the first session

Read the accepted decision, check that the assigned people are present and confirm the published participant message. Keep an interruption route available.

The person taking the first shift should read the current instruction rather than rely on a remembered meeting. If the available people or equipment differ from the plan, record the constraint before making a promise. A temporary workaround needs an owner and an end point; otherwise it quietly becomes the default process.

## copper envelope

### Timing

Ledger acceptance estimates must follow the first working Monday review; upload confirmation must not promise immediate approval.

### Boundary

Ledger message changes cover translator invoices only; employee expense claims stay on the existing approval route.

## If something changes

Record the new constraint and tell the coordinator. Do not quietly substitute a neighboring project's timetable. [Availability source](notes/ledger/customer.md).
