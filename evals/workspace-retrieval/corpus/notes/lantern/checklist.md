# Lantern opening checklist

Tracking reference: LN-204. This checklist belongs to Lantern; it does not transfer approval to another project.

- [ ] Open the accepted approach and read its scope.
- [ ] Check the partner availability record against the participant message.
- [ ] Assign the first shift and its backup.
- [ ] Give participants a clear route if the process is interrupted.
- [ ] Record the agreed outcome measure after the trial.

## Handoff

The checklist coordinates a handoff, not an automatic authorization. The person receiving it should be able to find the current decision and the original constraint.

The person taking the first shift should read the current instruction rather than rely on a remembered meeting. If the available people or equipment differ from the plan, record the constraint before making a promise. A temporary workaround needs an owner and an end point; otherwise it quietly becomes the default process.
