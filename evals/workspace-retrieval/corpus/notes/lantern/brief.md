# Purpose

Lantern aims to make the evening ceramics exhibition welcoming to visitors who do not read the local language.

Lantern is coordinated by Ivo.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that visitors wait at a wall of text and miss the first gallery. The work concerns museum exhibition, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is visitors reaching the second gallery without asking for directions. Watch for a misleading improvement: a shorter queue can conceal visitors leaving without seeing the exhibition. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
