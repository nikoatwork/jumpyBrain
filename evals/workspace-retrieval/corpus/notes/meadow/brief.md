# Purpose

Meadow aims to keep shared garden beds watered without depending on one retired volunteer every morning.

Meadow is coordinated by Ada.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that informal promises leave the same person covering missed watering turns. The work concerns urban garden volunteering, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is covered watering turns without unplanned rescue visits. Watch for a misleading improvement: an active group chat can conceal missed watering and unequal unpaid work. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
