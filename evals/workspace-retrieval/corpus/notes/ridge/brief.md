# Purpose

Ridge aims to plan a weekend walk that mixed-experience friends can finish together using public transport.

Ridge is coordinated by Tess.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that the most scenic circular route leaves slower walkers far from the last bus. The work concerns personal hiking club, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is all walkers back at the village stop before the final bus. Watch for a misleading improvement: the fastest walker finishing early says nothing about the whole group getting home. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
