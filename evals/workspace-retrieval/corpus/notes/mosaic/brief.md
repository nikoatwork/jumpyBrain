# Purpose

Mosaic aims to make room bookings accessible to neighbors who cannot stay on hold during working hours.

Mosaic is coordinated by Pia.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that telephone-only booking excludes people whose breaks are unpredictable. The work concerns community center appointments, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is room enquiries resolved within two offered callback windows. Watch for a misleading improvement: fewer incoming calls can indicate abandonment rather than successful booking. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
