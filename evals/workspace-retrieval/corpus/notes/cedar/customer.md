# External constraint

Cedar partner Elm College can verify applicant identities only on Fridays.

## What this record means

This is the availability the partner supplied for the current trial. It is not a promise that they will cover extra days, hours or activities. If the project changes, confirm availability again rather than carrying an old assumption into a new audience.

## Contact process

The coordinator owns follow-up; the people delivering the service should not be asked to negotiate a new agreement during a live session. Keep a written confirmation when the constraint changes.

The person taking the first shift should read the current instruction rather than rely on a remembered meeting. If the available people or equipment differ from the plan, record the constraint before making a promise. A temporary workaround needs an owner and an end point; otherwise it quietly becomes the default process.
