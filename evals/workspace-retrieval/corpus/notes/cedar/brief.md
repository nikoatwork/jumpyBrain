# Purpose

Cedar aims to let applicants without a printer complete a community scholarship application.

Cedar is coordinated by Noor.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that a scanned handwritten signature makes otherwise eligible applicants miss the deadline. The work concerns scholarship administration, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is eligible phone-only applicants submitting a complete application. Watch for a misleading improvement: raw submission volume can rise while eligible applicants are still excluded. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
