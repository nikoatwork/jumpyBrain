# Purpose

Forum aims to let remote speakers participate in a neighborhood conference without a full streaming crew.

Forum is coordinated by Leon.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that remote speakers cannot hear audience questions in the large hall. The work concerns small conference planning, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is remote speakers receiving and answering an intelligible audience question. Watch for a misleading improvement: stream view counts say little about whether remote guests can participate. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
