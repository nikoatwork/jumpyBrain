# Purpose

Harbor aims to help small charities finish account setup without booking a support call.

Harbor is coordinated by Mara.

## Problem worth solving

The current difficulty is that volunteers abandon setup when asked for a complete staff directory. The work concerns customer onboarding, not a general replacement for the surrounding organization. Success should be visible in an ordinary participant's day, rather than in the number of documents the project produces.

## How we will tell

The agreed measure is completed first task within two working days. Watch for a misleading improvement: a faster signup can look successful while new accounts never finish a useful task. Establish a small observation period and record counterexamples.

## Working relationship

The coordinator gathers feedback and maintains the source trail. Participation in a meeting does not make somebody an approver for every exception.
