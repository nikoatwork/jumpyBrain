# Desktop RAM follow-up — partial measurements, 2026-10-23

Six optimized runs completed: three repetitions each for 0 and 100 notes. Their source, driver, sampler, Electron/Node/QMD and model identities verified after measurement. All required applicable stages, complete semantic retrieval and observed descendant drain passed. [Matched aggregate evidence](2026-10-23-comparison-0-100.json) includes physical footprint, separate whole-stage RSS, active sampled peaks, CPU and latency ranges. Acceptance for 1,000 and 10,000 notes remains pending; no numerical budget is adopted.

These smaller corpora do not demonstrate a whole-desktop RAM reduction. At 100 notes, home settled physical footprint ranged from 351–360 MB in the control and 353–362 MB in the optimized runs. Recovery ranged from 377–379 MB versus 373–380 MB. Both ranges overlap. Graph-exit footprint was higher in the optimized group: 421–433 MB versus 404–415 MB. Semantic-index active sampled peaks remained approximately 5.09–5.32 GB versus 5.25–5.32 GB. These are decimal units and ranges of three per-run statistics, not pooled sample ranges or absolute peaks.

The 100 MB total target remains unsupported. Even the optimized empty-corpus background stage measured 114–123 MB; its open home stage measured 334–349 MB. Model-active and open/closed UI modes need separate limits. Earlier process-only allocation improvements do not establish whole-desktop savings.

The suite then failed at the first 1,000-note edit: its old driver waited for an inserted marker that never appeared. That driver can admit an editable editor before the note is loaded, matching the previously identified readiness race. The canonical marker was absent and the sampler recorded an empty owned process tree after failure. This attempt is excluded. The six completed runs were retained only after independent artifact verification and selected-corpus report/comparison guards passed.

A new control and optimized 1,000-note pair is queued with the same corrected loaded/visible-editor and explicit-deadline HTTP driver on both sides, plus the same gateway transport fix. The 10,000-note optimized suite follows against its existing corrected control. Old and corrected driver results must remain separate matched groups. The first queue stopped on failure and did not start its large suite.

Limits remain substantial: this measures the frozen first-iteration runtime, with warm/uncontrolled filesystem caches, an unrelated installed app still running, and user/focus/GPU noise. External minute-level observations recorded AC power and normal memory pressure during the completed runs. Short tooling tests also ran outside the owned tree during early empty-corpus recovery. Three repetitions on one machine do not establish a universal bound or leak freedom. The soak, GPU trace and matched native-host experiments remain open in the [active task](../../tasks/todo/tasks-desktop-ram-follow-up.md).
